### Notes on reading this model

- **Use the fully loaded CAC.** Most early selling is done by the founder. Cash CAC leaves that time out, so the loaded figure prices it at 18 hours × £45 per client won.
- **Pilot pricing:** the first 3 clients pay 50% of the setup fee in return for a case study.
- **Setup fees front-load revenue.** Early months look healthier than recurring revenue alone suggests. Track MRR separately, because that is what makes the company valuable and stable.
- **Churn is the most sensitive input.** Moving monthly churn from 3% to 5% cuts expected client lifetime from about 33 months to 20 months. Re-run the model with your real churn after month 6.
- **Startup costs** of £5,200 are deducted before month 1 in the cumulative cash line.
