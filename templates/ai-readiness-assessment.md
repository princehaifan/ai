# AI Readiness Assessment: questionnaire, scoring and report structure

> **Product:** £495 (single site) / £1,250 (multi-site), credited against any package within 30 days.
> **Format:** half a day on site or remote: 60-minute owner interview, 30-minute walk-through with front-of-house staff, systems review, then the report within 5 working days.

---

## Part 1: Questionnaire

### A. Business and goals

1. Business type, sites, staff numbers, opening hours
2. Revenue model: average transaction value, repeat rate, peak seasons
3. Top three goals for the next 12 months
4. Top three operational frustrations

### B. Customer contact and demand

5. Channels used by customers (phone, web form, email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, portals, walk-in), with rough volume per week for each
6. Missed or unanswered contacts per week (from call logs where possible)
7. Response time to messages and web enquiries (test it)
8. Most frequently asked questions (top 20)
9. Out-of-hours demand

### C. Bookings, sales and retention

10. Booking or sales system; how bookings are created and changed
11. No-show and late-cancellation rate; deposit policy
12. Follow-up, rebooking, reactivation and review processes
13. Marketing consent records (email, SMS, WhatsApp)

### D. Back office

14. Repetitive admin tasks and hours per week (emails, data entry, documents, invoices, rotas, reporting)
15. Documents processed (types, volumes)
16. Reporting: what the owner looks at, how often, how it's produced

### E. Systems and data

17. Software inventory: booking, CRM, POS, accounting, email, phone or VoIP, website platform
18. API or integration availability for each (check vendor documentation)
19. Data quality: are customer records complete and deduplicated?
20. Who administers the systems? Are admin logins shared?

### F. People and change

21. Staff digital confidence (1–5)
22. Current informal AI use (ChatGPT etc.). Is there a policy?
23. Concerns from staff or the owner about AI
24. Potential champion

### G. Risk and compliance

25. Personal data handled: any special category data (health), children's data, financial data?
26. Sector regulation (CQC, SRA, FCA, allergens, etc.)
27. Existing privacy notice, DPO or data lead, cyber security measures (MFA, backups, Cyber Essentials)
28. Customers in the EU (EU AI Act transparency relevance)

## Part 2: Readiness scoring (0–4 for each dimension)

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Opportunity size** | No measurable pain | Moderate, some volume | Large, measurable losses | |
| **Process clarity** | Undocumented, inconsistent | Partly consistent | Clear, repeatable processes | |
| **Systems and integrations** | Paper or closed systems | Some integrations | Modern systems with APIs | |
| **Data quality** | Poor or none | Usable with cleaning | Clean, structured | |
| **People and change** | Resistant, no champion | Open, limited time | Champion and enthusiasm | |
| **Governance and risk** | No privacy or security basics | Partial | Policies, MFA, privacy notice | |
| **Budget and authority** | No budget or decision-maker unclear | Some budget | Owner engaged, budget allocated | |

**Total /28:**

- **0–10, "Foundations first":** fix basics (systems, data, policies). Offer training and a small Starter.
- **11–19, "Quick wins ready":** Starter or Growth.
- **20–28, "Transformation ready":** Growth plus roadmap, or Transformation.

---

## Part 3: Opportunity prioritisation matrix

Score each opportunity found from 1 to 5.

| Opportunity | Value (1–5) | Ease (1–5) | Risk (5 = low) | Time to value (5 = fast) | **Priority = V × E × R × T** | Package |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. Missed-call text-back + AI receptionist | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 400 | Growth |
| e.g. Reminder and deposit flow | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 500 | Starter |
| e.g. Demand forecasting | 3 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 48 | Transformation (later) |

---

## Part 4: Report structure (8–12 pages)

1. **Executive summary:** readiness score, top three opportunities, estimated value range, recommended next step
2. **Where you are today:** customer journey map, channel volumes, baseline metrics
3. **Opportunities:** each with problem, proposed solution, estimated impact (ROI calculator, low/mid/high), effort, risks
4. **Readiness scorecard** with explanations
5. **Roadmap:** 0–3 months (quick wins), 3–6 months, 6–12 months
6. **Compliance and risk notes:** DPIA need, data flows, disclosures, sector rules
7. **People and training plan**
8. **Investment options:** recommended package, with alternatives
9. **Appendix:** systems inventory, FAQ list collected, assumptions

**Close the assessment** with a 45-minute presentation meeting, not just an emailed PDF. Present, discuss and agree the next step.
