# Discovery call script: free AI Opportunity Snapshot (30 minutes)

> **Purpose:** understand the business, quantify one or two costly problems, qualify (budget, authority, need, timeline) and agree a next step: a paid assessment, a personalised demo or a proposal.
> **Rule:** listen 70%, talk 30%. Don't pitch features until minute 20.

---

## 0. Before the call (10 minutes of research)

- [ ] Website, services and prices
- [ ] Google reviews: themes, unanswered reviews, complaints about phones or response times
- [ ] Booking, CRM or POS system (check booking links; use Wappalyzer)
- [ ] Opening hours vs. when customers are likely to enquire
- [ ] Social media activity and DMs
- [ ] Any test done (after-hours call, web enquiry response time)
- [ ] Three personalised observations written down

---

## 1. Opening (2 minutes)

> "Thanks for your time, [Name]. The aim of the next 30 minutes is to understand how [Business] handles customers and admin, spot one or two places where time or revenue is slipping, and tell you honestly whether AI can help. If it can't, I'll say so. Sound good? Is it OK if I take notes?"

## 2. The business (5 minutes)

1. "Tell me about [Business]: how long, how many staff, how many sites?"
2. "What does a typical customer spend, and how often do they come back?"
3. "What are your goals for the next 12 months?"
4. "What takes up most of your time that you wish it didn't?"

## 3. Customer journey and pain (10 minutes)

**Enquiries**

5. "How do new customers usually get in touch: phone, website, WhatsApp, Instagram, portals?"
6. "Roughly how many calls or messages a day? When are the peaks?"
7. "What happens when nobody can answer? Voicemail? Does anyone call back? How quickly?"
8. "Do you know how many calls you miss? Could we check your phone system's call log together?" *(Many VoIP systems show missed calls. This is the baseline.)*

**Bookings and attendance**

9. "How do bookings get into your system?"
10. "How often do people not turn up or cancel late? What does that cost you?"
11. "Do you take deposits or send reminders today?"

**Follow-up, retention and reviews**

12. "What happens after a customer visits? Any follow-up, rebooking or review requests?"
13. "Who replies to reviews, and how quickly?"

**Admin**

14. "Which repetitive admin tasks eat staff time: emails, forms, chasing documents, data entry?"

## 4. Quantify (5 minutes): fill in the ROI calculator live

> "Let's put rough numbers on this, using your figures, not mine."

| Input | Their answer |
|---|---|
| Missed calls or unanswered enquiries per week | |
| % of those that are new business | |
| % of new enquiries that normally become customers | |
| Average value of a new customer (first visit, or lifetime) | |
| No-shows per week × average booking value | |
| Staff hours per week on repetitive admin × hourly cost | |

→ See `templates/roi-calculator.md` for the formulas. **Say clearly that these are estimates from their numbers, not a promise.**

## 5. Qualify (3 minutes)

- **Authority:** "Apart from you, who else would be involved in a decision like this?"
- **Budget:** "If we could reliably fix [problem], what would that be worth to you each month? For context, our entry package starts at £195 a month plus setup."
- **Timeline:** "Is this something you'd want sorted in the next month, or more of a 'later this year'?"
- **Systems:** "Which booking or CRM system do you use? Can you add users to it?"
- **Concerns:** "What would worry you about using AI with your customers?"

## 6. Show (3 minutes)

Play or call the niche demo. Show a sample monthly report.

> "Imagine this answering as [Business], with your prices, hours and policies, and handing anything tricky to [champion name]."

## 7. Next step (2 minutes)

Choose one:

| Situation | Next step |
|---|---|
| Simple, clear single-channel pain, owner ready | "I'll build you a quick personalised demo and send a proposal by [date]. Can we look at it together on [day]?" |
| Multiple problems, several systems, or higher budget | "The best next step is our AI Readiness Assessment: half a day, £495, fully credited if you go ahead within 30 days. You get a prioritised roadmap with costs and expected impact either way." |
| Not a fit (no budget, no pain, no API, wrong scale) | "Honestly, I don't think we're the right fit right now. Here are two free things you could try: [e.g. Google Business Profile messaging, booking-link auto-reply]. Mind if I check in in six months?" |

**Always:** book the next meeting in the diary before you hang up.

---

## After the call (within 24 hours)

- [ ] CRM updated: stage, notes, numbers, BANT, next step
- [ ] Follow-up email with a summary of what you heard, the estimated opportunity range and the agreed next step
- [ ] If proposal: personalised demo built and proposal ready within 48 hours
