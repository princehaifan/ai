### Appendix: definitions and method

- **Bookings created by AI:** bookings tagged with source "Ostler AI" in [booking system].
- **Calls answered %:** answered calls ÷ total inbound calls (phone system data).
- **Staff hours saved:** conversations fully resolved by AI × average handling time agreed at baseline ([3] minutes).
- **Estimated value:** bookings attributed to AI × average booking value £[x] (from your POS). Excludes bookings that staff would have taken anyway, during staffed hours, where identifiable.
