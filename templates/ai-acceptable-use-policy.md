**[Business name]: Using AI tools at work**

## 1. Why we have this policy

AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and the AI assistants Ostler set up for us) can save time and help us serve customers better. They can also make mistakes and create privacy, security and reputational risks. This policy explains how we use them safely.

## 2. Approved tools

| Tool | Approved use | Account type |
|---|---|---|
| [Customer AI assistant (Ostler)] | Answering customer enquiries and bookings | Managed by Ostler |
| [e.g. ChatGPT Team / Claude Team / Copilot] | Drafting emails, summarising documents, ideas | **Business account only** (not personal free accounts) |

Ask [Name] before using any other AI tool for work.

## 3. The golden rules

1. **Never paste personal data** about customers, patients or staff (names with health details, addresses, dates of birth, financial details) into AI tools that aren't approved for it.
2. **Never paste confidential business information** (passwords, contracts, financial accounts, legal matters) into unapproved tools.
3. **Check everything.** AI can be confidently wrong. You are responsible for anything you send, publish or act on.
4. **Humans decide.** Don't use AI to make decisions about people (hiring, disciplinary action, refusing service, prioritising patients) without proper human review.
5. **Be honest.** Don't present AI-written content in a misleading way. Never use AI to write fake reviews or testimonials (illegal under the DMCC Act 2024).
6. **Respect copyright.** Don't ask AI to copy others' work. Check images and text before publishing.
7. **No advice in regulated areas** (medical, legal, financial) generated by AI without qualified professional review.
8. **Report problems.** If an AI tool or our assistant gives a wrong, offensive or risky answer, tell [Name] the same day.

## 4. Our customer-facing AI assistant

- It always tells customers they're speaking to an AI assistant, and offers a person.
- It only answers from information we've approved. **Tell [Name] when prices, menus, hours or policies change**, so it can be updated.
- Handovers from the assistant must be picked up within [timeframe].

## 5. Security

- Use multi-factor authentication on all AI tool accounts.
- Don't share logins.
- Log out of shared devices.

## 6. Training

All staff complete [Ostler AI literacy session / government AI Skills Hub foundation course] within [30] days of starting.

## 7. Breaches of this policy

Breaches may lead to [disciplinary action as set out in the staff handbook]. If you've made a mistake, such as pasting personal data, tell [Name] immediately. Fast reporting matters more than blame.
