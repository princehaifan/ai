# Outreach scripts

> Personalise every message. Check the PECR rules in `blueprint/08` §8.2 before any email, SMS or call:
> - Cold email is only for **limited companies and LLPs**, and must include an opt-out.
> - Don't cold email **sole traders or partnerships**.
> - Screen calls against **TPS and CTPS**.
> - Never use AI voice for outbound marketing.

---

## 1. Walk-in visit (20–40 seconds; outside peak hours)

> "Hi, I'm [Name] from Ostler, just down the road in [Town]. I won't keep you. I help local [restaurants/agents/clinics] stop missing customers when the team's too busy to answer the phone. Is the owner or manager around? … No worries. Could I leave this card? There's a demo number on it. Ring it any time and you'll hear an AI receptionist book a table at a pretend restaurant. If it's interesting, the QR code books a free 30-minute check of where you might be missing enquiries. When's usually a quiet time to catch [owner's name]?"

**After the visit:** log it in the CRM with the decision-maker's name, best time and any observations. Follow up within 3 working days by phone (after TPS/CTPS check) or LinkedIn.

---

## 2. LinkedIn connection note (no pitch)

> "Hi [First name], I work with [niche] businesses around [Town] on practical ways to handle enquiries faster. I'd enjoy following what you're doing at [Business]. Happy to connect."

### LinkedIn follow-up (after they accept and you've engaged with a post)

> "Thanks for connecting, [First name]. Quick question, no pitch: when [Business] gets enquiries after hours or during the busy rush, what usually happens to them? I'm running free 30-minute checks for a few local [niche] businesses this month to show where enquiries slip through. Happy to do one for you if useful."

---

## 3. Cold email: limited companies and LLPs only

**Subject:** Your 9pm enquiries, [Business name]

> Hi [First name],
>
> I sent an enquiry through [Business]'s website at 9:10pm on Tuesday as part of a short local study of [niche] response times. The reply came at [time] the next day. *(Only include this if you genuinely did it. Otherwise use an honest observation, e.g. "I noticed several Google reviews mention it's hard to get through by phone.")*
>
> That's normal, because your team is busy with customers. But people who enquire in the evening often book with whoever replies first.
>
> I run Ostler, a [Town]-based company that sets up AI assistants to answer and book enquiries instantly, 24/7, connected to systems like [their booking system], with a human handover for anything tricky.
>
> Would a free 30-minute check of where enquiries slip through be useful? Here's a 60-second demo: [link].
>
> Best,
> [Name] · Ostler Ltd · [address] · [phone]
>
> *I'm contacting you because your business is a [niche] in [area] and this seemed relevant. If you'd rather not hear from me, reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.*

**Follow-up 1 (4 days later):** "Bumping this in case it got buried. Here's what the AI receptionist sounds like answering a [niche] call: [demo number]. Worth 30 minutes?"
**Follow-up 2 (7 days later):** "Last note from me. If timing's wrong, no problem. I'll be at [local event] on [date] if you'd like to see it live."
**Then stop.** Move them to the 90-day nurture list.

---

## 4. Phone call (after a TPS/CTPS check, or a follow-up after a visit)

> "Hi, is that [Name]? It's [Your name] from Ostler. I popped in on [day] and left a card with [staff member]. Have you got two minutes, or is there a better time?
>
> I'll be brief. I help [niche] businesses capture the calls and messages that come in when the team's busy. Can I ask: roughly how many calls a day do you get, and what happens when nobody can pick up?
>
> [Listen. Note the numbers.]
>
> That's really common. What I'd suggest is a free 30-minute check where I look at your call and enquiry patterns and show you what an AI receptionist would sound like for [Business]. Would [day] or [day] work?"

---

## 5. Warm network message (Wave 0)

> "Hi [Name], hope you're well! I've just started a business helping local firms use AI to stop missing customers: things like answering calls when they're busy, WhatsApp booking assistants and fewer no-shows. I'm taking on **three pilot clients at half price** in exchange for honest feedback and a case study.
>
> Do you know any business owners who struggle with missed calls or slow replies? Or is that you? 😊 An intro would mean a lot."

---

## 6. Partner outreach (web agency / MSP / accountant)

> "Hi [Name], I run Ostler, a [Town] company that implements practical AI for local businesses: AI receptionists, WhatsApp booking assistants and admin automation. Several of your clients probably fit the profile.
>
> I don't do websites, IT support or accounts, so I'm not a competitor. I'm looking for two or three partners where we refer work to each other, with a [15%] recurring referral fee or a white-label option for you.
>
> Could I buy you a coffee and show you a live demo? I'm also happy to run a free 45-minute AI lunch-and-learn for your team."

---

## 7. Referral ask (at the 30-day results review)

> "I'm really pleased with how the first month has gone: [result]. Most of our clients come through recommendations. Who's one other business owner you know who has the same problem with [calls/no-shows/enquiries]? If you're happy to, a quick WhatsApp introduction works best, and you'll get a month free when they go live."
