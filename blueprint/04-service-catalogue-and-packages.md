> Covers deliverables 6 (Complete service catalogue) and 7 (Productised packages and pricing), plus strategic responsibilities 6 and 7.
> All prices exclude VAT. Ostler must register for VAT once taxable turnover passes £90,000 in a rolling 12 months [F, S20]. In the realistic model that happens in about month 13–14. Keep VAT-registered list prices the same and let VAT apply on top. Most target clients are VAT-registered and can reclaim it.

## 4.1 Pricing principles

1. **Anchor on value, price within market bands.** Every proposal shows the cost of the problem (from the ROI calculator) next to the price. Prices sit in the managed mid-market band found in research [E, S6, S7].
2. **Setup fee plus monthly fee, always.** The setup fee covers build effort and filters out unserious buyers. The monthly fee covers platform costs, monitoring, optimisation and reporting. **No project without a monthly plan.**
3. **Fixed prices, published.** Small businesses fear open-ended day rates. Publish "from" prices on the website.
4. **Usage is passed through fairly.** Each plan includes a usage allowance (voice minutes, messages). Above it, usage is billed at cost plus 20%, with alerts at 80% of the allowance.
5. **No long lock-in, with an incentive to commit.** Monthly rolling after a 3-month minimum, or 10% off the monthly fee for a 12-month term.
6. **Assessment credit.** The £495 AI Readiness Assessment fee is credited in full against any package ordered within 30 days.
7. **Pilot pricing (first three clients per niche only):** 50% off setup in return for a written case study, a testimonial, a referral introduction and permission to use anonymised metrics.
8. **Never guarantee revenue or savings.** Commit to delivery, service levels and transparent measurement, not outcomes outside your control.

## 4.5 How success will be measured (pricing and packaging)

| Metric | Target | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal-to-close rate | ≥ 35% | Monthly |
| Share of deals closed at list price (excluding pilots) | ≥ 80% | Monthly |
| Assessment-to-package conversion | ≥ 60% | Monthly |
| Starter-to-Growth upgrade within 6 months | ≥ 25% | Quarterly |
| Gross margin by package | ≥ 65% | Monthly |
| Price objection cited as main loss reason | < 20% of losses | Quarterly |

**Price review:** after ten closed deals, raise prices by 10–15% if close rate exceeds 50%. Hold them if close rate sits at 25–50%. Below 25%, re-examine positioning and demo quality before cutting price.
