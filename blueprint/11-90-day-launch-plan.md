# 11. 90-day launch plan

> Covers deliverable 16 (90-day launch plan) and Execution Roadmap Phases 1–3.
> Assumes one full-time founder. If you're part-time, stretch it to 130–150 days and keep the order.
> **Day 0 = the day you commit.** Each week ends with a 30-minute review: what moved the numbers, and what stops next week.

---

## Overview

| Phase | Days | Theme | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Foundation** | 1–28 | Validate, choose niches, set up the company, build demos, set prices | 20 validation conversations; 2 niches chosen; 3 working demos; company and legal basics done |
| **Phase 2: Market entry** | 29–56 | Website, pipeline, outreach, pilots | Website live; 200 prospects per niche in CRM; 3 pilots signed |
| **Phase 3: First revenue** | 57–90 | Deliver pilots, measure, convert, sell at list price | 3 pilots live with baselines; first case study; first list-price client; ≥ £600 MRR |

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (days 1–28)

### Week 1: Validate the problem and choose niches

| Task | Detail | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Pull your local market data | Nomis UK Business Counts by SIC code for your area (`blueprint/02` §2.2) | Market sheet |
| Score niches | Apply the scoring matrix, adjusted for **your own network and experience** | 2 launch niches selected (provisional) |
| Book 20 validation conversations | Warm network first: owners and managers in the two niches. **Not selling, learning.** | 20 meetings booked across weeks 1–3 |
| Set up basics | Business email, Google Workspace, HubSpot free CRM, Notion, 1Password with MFA everywhere | Working tools |

**Validation conversation questions:** How do enquiries reach you? What happens when you can't answer? How many calls or messages a day? What does a new customer or booking typically spend? What have you tried? What would make you pay for a fix? Who decides? *(Full script: `templates/discovery-call-script.md`.)*

### Week 2: Company and legal foundation

| Task | Output | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Check name (Companies House, IPO, domain), then incorporate | Ltd company, domain | ~£100 + £30 |
| Business bank account; accountant appointed | Account; accountant | ~£120/month |
| ICO fee | Registration | £52 |
| Insurance quotes (PI, cyber, PL) | Policies in place before the first client | ~£110/month |
| Instruct a solicitor on templates (MSA, SOW, DPA, privacy notice, T&Cs) | Drafts due by week 5 | ~£1,200 |
| Continue validation conversations (8+ done) | Notes in CRM | — |

### Week 3: Build demos (part 1)

| Task | Output |
|---|---|
| Set up core stack: n8n (cloud to start, or self-hosted), Supabase, Langfuse, Retell or Vapi, Twilio, WhatsApp Cloud API test number (`blueprint/05`) | Working stack |
| **Demo 1: missed-call text-back + AI voice receptionist** for niche A, with a fictional business, realistic FAQs and a calendar booking | Live demo number |
| Validation conversations (15+ done) | Pattern of pains and willingness to pay noted |

### Week 4: Demos (part 2), pricing and brand

| Task | Output |
|---|---|
| **Demo 2: WhatsApp and web booking assistant** for niche B, with booking integration in a sandbox | Live demo |
| **Demo 3: Monthly results report** mock-up (dashboard and PDF) | Sample report |
| Finalise packages and pricing; test them on the last 5 validation conversations ("If this cost £195 a month plus £950 setup, would it be worth it?") | Confirmed price list (adjust ±20% if needed) |
| Brand basics: name confirmed, logo, colours, one-page package sheets, leave-behind card with QR code | Brand kit |
| **Go/no-go review:** did ≥ 30% of validation contacts say they'd consider paying? Is one niche clearly stronger? | Decision recorded |

**Phase 1 success measures:** 20 conversations; at least 6 people say "I'd pay for that"; 3 demos working end to end; legal and insurance in progress; total spend ≤ £3,500.

### Week 5: Website and assets

| Task | Output |

### Week 6: Build the pipeline and start Wave 0

| Task | Output |
|---|---|
| Build prospect lists: 200 per niche, scored | CRM populated |
| **Wave 0:** 50 personal messages to warm contacts asking for introductions and pilot candidates (`blueprint/08` §8.7) | 10+ conversations |
| Begin walk-ins: 15 visits (outside peak hours), leave cards with the demo number | Snapshots booked |

### Week 7: Snapshots and proposals

| Task | Output |
|---|---|
| Run 6–8 free snapshots; capture baseline data (call logs, response-time tests) | Qualified opportunities |
| Build personalised demos for the best 3–4 | Demos |
| Present 3–4 pilot proposals with written success plans | Proposals out |
| Run the "7pm Test" (hospitality) or "9pm Enquiry Test" (property) research for niche B campaign content | Campaign data |

### Week 8: Sign pilots

| Task | Output |
|---|---|
| Close **3 pilots** (50% off setup, normal monthly fee from go-live; case study and referral commitments) | 3 signed |
| Kick-off meetings; onboarding forms; access requests; DPIA screening | Onboarding started |
| Contact 10 potential partners (web agencies, MSPs, accountants) | 3 partner meetings booked |
| Publish campaign results on LinkedIn (aggregate, anonymised, measured figures only) | Content live |

**Phase 2 success measures:** website, GBP and LinkedIn live; 400 prospects in CRM; 30+ conversations; 8+ snapshots; **3 pilots signed**; ≥ £700 in setup deposits collected (50% of three pilot setup fees).

## Daily non-negotiables (from week 6)

1. **10 new prospect touches a day:** visits, calls, LinkedIn messages or introductions.
2. **One piece of visible proof a week:** a post, demo video or result.
3. **Update the CRM the same day.**
4. **Protect two build days a week**, so delivery never slips.

---

## Risks in the first 90 days and responses

| Risk | Early signal | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Can't book validation or snapshot meetings | < 10 conversations by day 21 | Shift to more walk-ins and warm introductions; sharpen the opener around one specific pain; test the other niche |
| Prospects like it but won't pay | "Interesting" but no pilots by day 50 | Make the pilot cheaper on setup only (keep the monthly fee); offer a 30-day opt-out; check you're talking to decision-makers |
| Integration blocked (no API) | Booking system lacks API access | Use the vendor's native integrations, calendar sync, email parsing or booking links; avoid that system for pilots |
| Pilot results weak | Day-14 metrics flat | Check that call forwarding or the widget is actually live and visible; tune scripts; add the reminder flow for a quick win |
| Founder overload | Builds slipping | Use templates; hire a freelancer for overflow; cap pilots at 3 |
