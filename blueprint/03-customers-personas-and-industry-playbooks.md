## 3.1 Segmentation: who to serve first

### Ideal customer profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Ideal | Avoid (for now) |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 5–100 staff, £300k–£10m turnover | Sole traders with no staff; enterprises with procurement teams |
| Decision-maker | Owner-operator or managing director who can say yes in one or two meetings | Committee decisions or head-office approval |
| Operating model | Bookings, appointments or inbound enquiries drive revenue | Low-touch, low-volume businesses |
| Pain signal | Missed calls, slow replies, no-shows, admin overload, unanswered reviews | "Just curious about AI" with no specific problem |
| Systems | Uses a booking, CRM, POS or practice system with an API or integrations | Paper diaries only (possible later, but harder) |
| Customer value | Each new customer or booking is worth £50+ (or high lifetime value) | Very low-value, impulse transactions |
| Attitude | Open to change, values time, pays for services | Wants it free or wants to haggle every line |

### Recommendation: two launch niches plus a fast-follow

**The "unfair advantage" rule:** if you already have contacts, experience or credibility in one industry, start there. A warm network beats a higher score. Without that, choose as follows:

| Phase | Niche | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Launch niche A** | **Estate and lettings agents** *or* **trades and home services** (pick the one where you have more contacts) | Highest scores. Every missed enquiry is a lost instruction or job worth hundreds to thousands of pounds. Owners decide quickly. Lower regulatory risk. |
| **Launch niche B** | **Independent hospitality**: restaurants with 60+ covers, plus small hotels | A priority in the brief. Very visible locally, so case studies spread by word of mouth. Simple, well-documented booking integrations. Target the upper tier that can pay. |
| **Fast-follow (months 4–9)** | Salons and aesthetics, gyms, dental and private clinics | Reuse the booking-assistant playbook. Bring in clinics once the compliance pack is proven (special category health data). |
| **Later (months 9–18)** | Accountants, law firms, car dealers, retail | Higher-value Transformation work: document automation, internal knowledge bases, analytics |
| **Partner-led only** | NHS organisations, pharmacies | Long sales cycles and strict standards (DSPT, DCB0129/0160) [S18]. Enter through an established, NHS-assured supplier. |

**Why only two niches?** Each niche needs its own demo, landing page, vocabulary, integrations and case study. Two lets you compare and learn. Five spreads you too thin to become known for anything.

## 3.2 Customer personas

Personas are **composites [A]**. Validate and refine them from your first 20 discovery calls.

### Persona 1 — "Daniel", estate agency director

- **Business:** independent sales and lettings agency, two branches, 14 staff, uses Reapit or Alto.
- **Goals:** win more instructions, book more valuations and viewings, keep landlords happy.
- **Frustrations:** portal enquiries arriving at 9pm get answered the next morning, by which time a rival has booked the viewing. Negotiators spend hours on repetitive tenant questions and chasing references.
- **Buying triggers:** losing a valuation to a competitor; a negotiator leaving; a slow month.
- **Objections:** "Our clients want a human." "We tried a chatbot and it was useless."
- **What wins him:** a live demo answering a real listing question on WhatsApp and booking a viewing into his diary in under 30 seconds, plus a speed-to-lead report.
- **Package fit:** Growth.

### Persona 2 — "Priya", restaurant owner-operator

- **Business:** 80-cover independent restaurant, 22 staff, uses ResDiary or OpenTable, an EPOS system and Instagram.
- **Goals:** fuller tables midweek, fewer no-shows, less time on the phone during service.
- **Frustrations:** the phone rings during the 7pm rush and nobody can answer. No-shows on Saturdays. Reviews pile up unanswered.
- **Buying triggers:** a bad no-show weekend; a negative review that went unanswered; staff shortages.
- **Objections:** "Margins are tight." "It'll sound robotic to my guests."
- **What wins her:** hearing the AI receptionist take a booking in a natural British voice, handle allergen FAQs from her own menu and hand over to staff; a reminder and deposit flow; a cost comparison with a part-time host.
- **Package fit:** Starter, upgrading to Growth.

### Persona 3 — "Mark", trades business owner

- **Business:** plumbing and heating company, 9 engineers, office manager, uses Jobber, ServiceM8 or Tradify.
- **Goals:** never miss a job enquiry, fill the engineers' diaries, get quotes out faster.
- **Frustrations:** calls go unanswered while he is on site. Emergency callers ring the next number on Google. Quotes take days to send.
- **Buying triggers:** a quiet week; the office manager on holiday; a Google ranking drop.
- **Objections:** "I'm not techy." "How much is this going to cost me?"
- **What wins him:** a missed-call text-back plus an AI receptionist that qualifies the job, gives a call-out price range he has approved and books into his job system; a weekly WhatsApp summary.
- **Package fit:** Starter, then Growth.

### Persona 4 — "Helen", boutique hotel general manager

- **Business:** 28-room independent hotel with a restaurant and events, uses a property management system and channel manager.
- **Goals:** more direct bookings (fewer OTA commissions), happier guests, a smaller night-shift burden.
- **Frustrations:** repetitive pre-arrival questions, multilingual guests, after-hours calls, and staff time lost to email.
- **Objections:** "Guest experience is everything. I can't risk a bad answer."
- **What wins her:** a multilingual concierge assistant on her website and WhatsApp that answers from her own information pack and escalates to the duty manager, plus a direct-booking conversion report.
- **Package fit:** Growth.

### Persona 5 — "Dr Okafor", private dental practice owner (fast-follow)

- **Business:** 6-surgery private practice, practice manager, uses practice management software.
- **Goals:** convert new-patient enquiries, reduce failed appointments, free reception staff from the phone.
- **Frustrations:** reception is overwhelmed at 8:30am; new-patient calls go to voicemail; recalls are manual.
- **Objections:** "Patient data is sensitive. Is this GDPR compliant? What about the GDC and CQC?"
- **What wins her:** an **admin-only** scope (no clinical advice, with triage rules that route anything clinical to staff); a DPIA completed for her; UK or EU data hosting; clear human escalation; a fee comparison with an extra receptionist.
- **Package fit:** Growth, with a compliance add-on.

### Persona 6 — "Sam", practice or operations manager (the champion)

Often not the buyer, but the person who **makes the project succeed or fail**. Sam wants less firefighting and fears being replaced or blamed. **Win Sam by** positioning AI as taking repetitive tasks off the team, involving Sam in designing the flows, training Sam first, and putting Sam's wins in the monthly report.

## 3.3 Industry playbooks: problems and AI solutions

Each playbook lists the problems, the solution, typical integrations, the KPIs to baseline and track, and compliance notes. *Integration names are examples. Confirm each vendor's current API or integration options before you scope work.*

### A. Restaurants and cafés

| Problem | AI / automation solution | KPI to baseline and track |
|---|---|---|
| Phone unanswered during service | AI voice receptionist: bookings, opening hours, allergens FAQ, large-party enquiries handed to the manager | Answered call rate; bookings taken by AI |
| No-shows | Automated confirmations, reminders, deposits for large or peak bookings, waitlist backfill | No-show rate; covers recovered |
| Slow replies on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and web | Omnichannel booking and FAQ assistant | First response time; enquiry-to-booking conversion |
| Reviews unanswered | AI-drafted, human-approved review responses; post-visit review request flow | Review volume, average rating, response rate |
| Quiet midweek | Segmented SMS, WhatsApp and email campaigns to past guests, with consent | Campaign bookings; midweek covers |
| Upselling | Menu-aware recommendations in the booking assistant (wine, set menus, events) | Pre-orders and event enquiries |
| Waste and ordering | Demand forecasting from EPOS and booking data (Transformation tier) | Food cost %; waste logs |

- **Evidence of pain:** the average no-show costs about £89 per lost customer (Barclaycard, 2023) [E, S21]. Industry-wide figures are dated or contested, so **measure each restaurant's own no-show rate**.
- **Integrations:** ResDiary, OpenTable, SevenRooms, Collins; EPOS (Lightspeed, Square, Toast); Google Business Profile; Meta (Instagram and WhatsApp).
- **Compliance:** allergen answers must come only from the restaurant's verified allergen matrix, with a "please confirm with staff" handover for severe allergies. Marketing campaigns need consent (PECR). Never post or incentivise fake reviews (DMCC Act 2024) [F, S19].
- **Demo:** call a demo number, book a table for six on Friday, ask "is the risotto gluten free?" and receive a WhatsApp confirmation.

### B. Gyms and fitness centres

| Problem | Solution | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Membership enquiries go cold | Instant AI reply on web, Instagram and WhatsApp; tour booking; automated follow-up sequence | Lead-to-tour and tour-to-join conversion |
| Class booking admin | Assistant connected to the booking platform (book, cancel, waitlist) | Front-desk enquiries handled automatically |
| Member churn | Churn-risk flags from attendance drop-off with a personal re-engagement message (human-approved) | Monthly cancellation rate |
| Personalised engagement | Onboarding journeys, milestone messages, PT upsell | 90-day retention; PT sales |
| Failed payments | Polite automated dunning via WhatsApp and SMS | Recovered revenue |

- **Integrations:** Glofox, Mindbody, TeamUp, Gymcatch, ClubRight; Stripe and GoCardless.
- **Compliance:** health or injury information is special category data, so avoid collecting it in chat unless necessary and covered by a DPIA. Marketing consent is required. Churn prediction must not drive automated decisions with significant effects without human review [S15].

### C. Retail shops and supermarkets (independent)

| Problem | Solution | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Repetitive customer questions (stock, hours, orders, returns) | Web and WhatsApp assistant connected to the product catalogue and order status | Tickets deflected; response time |
| Stock-outs and overstock | Sales-based demand forecasting and reorder suggestions (Transformation) | Stock-out days; inventory turns |
| Generic marketing | Segmented campaigns from purchase history; product recommendations | Repeat purchase rate; campaign revenue |
| Unclear performance | AI-assisted sales dashboard with weekly plain-English summary | Owner time on reporting |

- **Integrations:** Shopify, WooCommerce, Square, Lightspeed, Epos Now; Klaviyo or Mailchimp.
- **Compliance:** consumer law on returns and prices (assistants must quote policy verbatim); PECR consent.

### D. Hotels and accommodation

| Problem | Solution | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-arrival and in-stay questions | Multilingual AI concierge (web, WhatsApp, SMS) answering from the hotel's knowledge base | Front-desk contact volume; guest satisfaction |
| After-hours calls | AI receptionist with duty-manager escalation | Answered call rate overnight |
| OTA commission | Direct-booking assistant on the website; abandoned enquiry follow-up | Direct booking share |
| Guest communications | Automated pre-arrival, upsell (late check-out, dinner), post-stay review flow | Upsell revenue; review score |
| Events and weddings enquiries | Qualification and brochure automation, sales follow-up | Enquiry-to-viewing conversion |

- **Integrations:** PMS (Mews, Cloudbeds, Guestline, Little Hotelier), channel managers, booking engines.
- **Compliance:** EU guests mean EU AI Act Article 50 transparency applies to the assistant (disclose that it is AI) [S17]. Cover international data transfers in the privacy notice.

### E. Healthcare: private clinics, dentists, physiotherapy (admin only)

| Problem | Solution | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Phone overload at opening | AI receptionist for booking, rescheduling, opening hours, prices and directions; anything clinical goes to staff | Call abandonment; staff phone hours |
| New-patient enquiries lost | Instant response and booking, follow-up for incomplete enquiries | Enquiry-to-appointment conversion |
| Failed appointments | Reminders, confirmations and easy rescheduling; waitlist backfill | Did-not-attend rate |
| Recalls and reactivation | Automated recall campaigns (hygiene, check-ups) | Recall booking rate |
| Admin and documents | Form intake, referral letter triage, document classification with human review | Admin hours; turnaround time |
| Reporting | Operational dashboards (utilisation, cancellations, revenue per chair) | Utilisation % |

- **Hard rules:**
  - **No clinical advice, diagnosis or triage decisions by AI.** Use scripted red-flag detection ("chest pain", "bleeding", "can't breathe") that immediately directs the person to 999, 111 or staff.
  - Health data is special category data. Complete a DPIA for every client, use UK or EU hosting where possible, and sign data processing agreements with every sub-processor.
  - Tools that perform clinical functions may be medical devices (MHRA) and, in NHS settings, require DCB0129/DCB0160 clinical safety work, DSPT and DTAC [S18]. **Keep the scope administrative** unless partnering with an assured supplier.
- **Integrations:** practice management systems (verify API access per vendor; some require partner programmes), Google Calendar, Twilio.

## 3.4 Universal "quick win" solutions (sell these first)

These five solutions apply across almost every niche. They are fast to deploy, easy to demonstrate and easy to measure. Build them as **reusable templates** before anything else.

1. **Missed-call text-back plus AI receptionist.** Never lose an inbound call.
2. **Omnichannel enquiry and booking assistant.** Covers web, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.
3. **Reminder, confirmation and no-show reduction flow.**
4. **Review generation and AI-drafted responses**, with a human approving every response.
5. **Lead follow-up and reactivation sequences.**

**Success measure for the playbooks:** each launch niche has a working demo, a landing page, a one-page case study and a standard scope within 90 days. Each template gets 30% faster to deploy by its fifth deployment.
