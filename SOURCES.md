# Sources and evidence register

Every market claim in this blueprint carries one of three labels:

| Label | Meaning | How to treat it |
|---|---|---|
| **[F]** Fact | Verified against an official or primary source listed below | Safe to quote, with the date |
| **[E]** Estimate | Built from published third-party benchmarks or vendor pages, which vary and are often marketing material | Use as a range; re-check before quoting to a client |
| **[A]** Assumption | Our own working figure, to be validated in the market | Test it in the first 90 days; replace it with real data |

Research was carried out in November 2026. Prices and regulations change. **Re-check anything you rely on commercially, and re-check every vendor price before you quote it.**

## Market size and AI adoption

- **[S1]** Department for Business and Trade, *Business population estimates for the UK and regions 2025: statistical release* (published 2 October 2025). 5.7 million private sector businesses at the start of 2025, of which 5,681,930 were SMEs (99.85%). About 4.27 million had no employees beyond the owner. SMEs account for about 60% of private sector employment and 51% of turnover. The release is labelled "official statistics in development".
  https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/business-population-estimates-2025/business-population-estimates-for-the-uk-and-regions-2025-statistical-release
- **[S2]** Office for National Statistics, *Artificial intelligence in UK businesses: 2023 to 2026* (BICS-based analysis, 2026). Reported coverage: adoption among businesses with 10+ employees rose from about 12% (late 2023) to about 35% (mid-2026), and adoption was described as widening rather than deepening. Note that ONS figures change with the size band used, so always quote the band.
  https://cy.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026
  Secondary commentary: https://www.techmarketview.com/news/archive/2026/07/21/ukhotviewsextra-uk-ai-adoption-has-tripled-since-2023-but-barely-deepened
- **[S3]** DSIT (IFF Research & Technopolis), *AI Adoption Research* (published 13 February 2026, fieldwork in 2025). About one in six businesses (5+ employees) were using AI; the most-cited barriers were perceived lack of need (71%) and lack of AI skills (60%); most adopters reported productivity gains but most had not yet seen revenue change.
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-adoption-research/ai-adoption-research
- **[S4]** DSIT, *AI adoption survey 2026: executive summary*. Reports roughly a third of UK businesses using at least one AI technology (32%, up from 16% in 2025).
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-adoption-survey-2026/ai-adoption-survey-report-2026-executive-summary
- **[S5]** Government free AI skills training (announced 28 January 2026): AI Skills Hub, benchmarked by Skills England; SMEs named as a priority group; target of 10 million workers upskilled by 2030. Reporting cites FSB research that 46% of small firms lack the skills or knowledge to use AI well (secondary source).
  https://www.uctoday.com/talent-hcm-platforms/uk-launches-free-ai-training-to-prepare-10-million-workers-for-the-future-economy/

## Legal and regulatory

- **[S14]** ICO data protection fee: £52 (tier 1), £78 (tier 2), £3,763 (tier 3) from 17 February 2025; £5 Direct Debit discount. https://www.gov.uk/data-protection-register-notify-ico-personal-data
- **[S15]** Data (Use and Access) Act 2025: reforms UK GDPR, including a new automated decision-making regime (Articles 22A–22D) that relaxes the general restriction except for special category data, with safeguards. Main provisions reported in force from 5 February 2026; complaints-handling duty from 19 June 2026. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/data-use-and-access-act-2025-data-protection-and-privacy-changes · commentary: https://bratby.law/data-use-and-access-act-2025-commencement/
- **[S16]** ICO, business-to-business marketing under PECR: the electronic-mail marketing rule does not apply to corporate subscribers (companies, LLPs); sole traders and some partnerships are individual subscribers and need consent or the soft opt-in. Live calls: CTPS covers companies, TPS covers sole traders and partnerships. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/
- **[S17]** EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties (chatbot disclosure) apply from 2 August 2026, relevant where a UK business deploys AI to people in the EU. https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/viewpoints/102nfqm/you-talkin-to-me-operationalising-the-eu-ai-acts-transparency-obligations
- **[S18]** NHS clinical safety standards DCB0129 (manufacturers) and DCB0160 (deploying organisations), plus DSPT and DTAC for NHS-connected tools. Summary for practices: https://www.wessexlmcs.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Guidance-on-the-use-of-AI-enabled-ambient-scribing-products-in-health-and-care-settings-A-Summary-.docx · Primary: https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/information-standards
- **[S19]** Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024: fake reviews and concealed incentivised reviews are banned unfair commercial practices (in force from 6 April 2025). https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/13 · CMA guidance: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fake-reviews-cma208
- **[S20]** VAT registration threshold: £90,000 taxable turnover in a rolling 12 months. https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration/when-to-register

### What we could not verify (and so did not claim)

- Moneypenny's and other human answering services' current prices.
- Specific UK WhatsApp per-message rates (use Meta's live rate card).
- Industry-wide missed-call rates for UK salons, clinics or trades. Most published figures come from vendors selling call-answering tools. **Measure each prospect's own missed calls instead** (see `templates/roi-calculator.md`).
- Churn benchmarks for UK AI automation retainers. The model uses assumptions (2–5% a month) that you should replace with your own data.
