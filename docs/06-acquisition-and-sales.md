## 6.1 Channel strategy: ranked by speed to first revenue

| Rank | Channel | Speed | Cost | Why / how |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warm network & founder referrals** | Days | £0 | Your first 1–3 clients will almost certainly come from people who already trust you. Contact 50–100 people personally in week 3 (script below). |
| 2 | **Targeted cold outreach (email + LinkedIn)** | 2–6 weeks | £100–£250/month data tools | Signal-based: contact companies with a *reason* (funding, AI job ads, support hiring, competitor AI launches). Never mass-blast. |
| 3 | **Agency partnerships** | 4–10 weeks | Referral fees | 3–5 software agencies can each send 1–4 AI projects a year. One partner can be worth more than 1,000 cold emails. |
| 4 | **Founder-led LinkedIn content** | 6–12 weeks to compound | Time | Builds trust so outbound gets replies. Proof-led posts, not opinions. |
| 5 | **VC / accelerator partnerships** | 2–4 months | Free workshops | Offer portfolio "AI office hours" + a discounted Sprint. One partnership gives warm access to dozens of funded companies. |
| 6 | **Workshops & webinars** | 1–2 months | Low | Co-host with partners (agencies, VCs, accountants' networks). Every session ends with a Sprint offer. |
| 7 | **Technical case studies** | After first delivery | Time | The most powerful asset you'll have. Each one feeds every other channel. |
| 8 | **Referral programme** | Ongoing | 10% of first project | For clients and contacts: 10% of the first project fee (or a donation to a charity of their choice) for introductions that close. |
| 9 | **SEO & organic** | 6–12 months | Time | Long-term. Focus on high-intent, problem-specific pages ("AI support automation for Zendesk", "AI document extraction for accountancy firms UK"), not "what is AI". |
| 10 | Paid ads | n/a | High | **Not in year one.** Expensive for consulting and attracts low-intent leads. |

### Partnerships playbook

| Partner type | Offer to them | What we get | Target in 90 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software dev agencies | White-label AI delivery or 15–20% referral fee; we never poach their clients (written non-solicit) | AI projects from their existing clients | 3 signed partners |
| VCs / accelerators / angel networks | Free portfolio workshop + "AI office hours"; 10% portfolio discount on Sprints | Warm intros to funded companies | 1 active partnership |
| MSPs / IT providers (for professional firms) | Referral fee; we handle AI, they keep infrastructure | Access to ICP 2, IT gatekeeper becomes an ally | 2 conversations → 1 partner |
| Accountants / fractional CFOs | Co-hosted "AI for finance teams" sessions | Trusted advisor introductions to SMEs | 1 co-hosted event |
| Complementary SaaS vendors (help desks, CRMs, automation platforms) | Implementation partner listing | Inbound leads from their customers | Apply to 2 partner programmes |

## 6.2 The 10-step repeatable sales process

| Step | What happens | Tools / assets | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Identify** | Build lists of ICP companies showing a **trigger signal**: recent funding, AI/ML job ads, support-team hiring, competitor AI launches, new COO/CTO | LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo (or similar), Crunchbase/Beauhurst, job boards | Company matches ICP + has a live signal |
| **2. Research** | 10–15 minutes per account: product, team size, support channels, tech stack (BuiltWith), public pain (reviews, job ads) | Research checklist; an AI research agent we build for ourselves (our own first case study) | 1–2 specific hypotheses written down |
| **3. Identify opportunities** | Map the hypothesis to one of our five problem areas. Estimate the value roughly | Opportunity hypothesis template | A one-line "I noticed X, it usually costs Y, we fix it with Z" |
| **4. Initiate contact** | Personalised email + LinkedIn connection; 3–4 touch sequence over 14 days; warm intro if available | Templates (below), CRM sequence | Reply or sequence complete |
| **5. Discovery meeting** | 30–45 minutes. Diagnose, don't pitch. Qualify budget, authority, need, timing, data access | Discovery script (below) | Qualified (score ≥ 12/20) or disqualified politely |
| **6. AI opportunity assessment** | For smaller deals: a free 1-page "Opportunity Snapshot" within 48 hours. For larger: sell the paid Sprint | Snapshot template; Sprint proposal | Client agrees to Sprint or direct Pilot |
| **7. Proposal** | Written within 3 business days. Problem in their words, baseline, acceptance metric, scope, exclusions, timeline, price options (good/better/best), Run plan included | Proposal template (below) | Proposal reviewed live on a call, never just emailed |
| **8. Close** | Handle objections, agree start date, sign MSA + SOW + DPA, invoice deposit | E-signature, contract templates | Signed + deposit paid |
| **9. Deliver** | Per delivery SOP ([08-operating-model-and-sops.md](08-operating-model-and-sops.md)). Weekly demo and status | Delivery kit, eval harness | Acceptance metric met, signed off |
| **10. Convert to recurring** | Run plan starts automatically. At week 4 of Run, present the value report and the next opportunity from the roadmap. Ask for the case study and referrals | Value report template, case-study release form | Run retainer active + next Build proposed + referral asked |

### Discovery qualification score (out of 20)

| Criterion | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pain**: cost of the problem | Vague | Real but unquantified | Quantified (£/hours/revenue) |
| **Authority** | No access to buyer | Influencer | Budget holder in the room |
| **Budget** | No budget | "Could find it" | Allocated / funded recently |
| **Timing** | "Someday" | This half-year | This quarter, with a reason |
| **Data & access** | No data / locked | Data exists, access unclear | Data and system access available |

≥ 16: propose a Pilot directly · 12–15: propose a Sprint · < 12: nurture (content, newsletter), check back in 90 days.

### Weekly targets (solo founder)

| Activity | Weeks 3–8 | Weeks 9–13 | Months 4–12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| New personalised outreach (email + LinkedIn) | 40–50 | 50 | 30–50 (falls as referrals and partners grow) |
| Follow-ups | All due | All due | All due |
| LinkedIn posts | 3 | 3 | 3 |

## 6.4 Founder-led LinkedIn content system

**Positioning on LinkedIn:** "The person who actually ships AI and shows the numbers."

| Pillar (share of posts) | Example post types |
|---|---|
| **Proof** (35%) | Build logs, before/after metrics, anonymised eval results, "what broke and how we fixed it" |
| **Teardowns** (25%) | "How I'd add AI to [public SaaS product]'s support flow", "Build vs buy: when Intercom Fin beats a custom agent" |
| **Practical how-to** (25%) | Evals explained simply, RAG pitfalls, cost control, AI policy templates (lead magnets) |
| **Point of view** (15%) | Why most AI pilots fail (cite MIT, carefully), what UK firms should do about the EU AI Act, hype vs reality |

**Lead magnets** (gated or ungated, offered in posts and outreach):
1. *AI Opportunity Scorecard*: a 20-question self-assessment (spreadsheet or web form) that scores readiness and suggests first use cases.
2. *UK AI Acceptable Use Policy template* for professional firms.
3. *The Production AI Checklist*: 25 checks before an AI feature goes live (evals, guardrails, cost, privacy).

## 6.5 Templates

### Warm network message (week 3)

> Hi [Name], quick personal update: I've started [Company], helping tech companies and professional firms get AI into production (not just pilots). We start with a 1–2 week diagnostic, then build one workflow in six weeks with the success metric agreed up front.
> I'm taking on three founding clients at a reduced rate in exchange for a case study. Is there anyone in your network (or your own team) wrestling with support volume, document-heavy processes or shipping AI features? An intro would mean a lot.

### Cold email: signal-based (ICP 1, CTO)

> **Subject:** [Company]'s AI roadmap, after the Series A
>
> Hi [Name],
> Congrats on the round. I noticed you're hiring an ML engineer and that [competitor] launched an AI assistant last month. Most teams I speak to in that spot have a prototype that works in demos but isn't reliable enough to ship.
> We help SaaS teams take one AI feature from prototype to production in six weeks: evals, guardrails and cost controls included, without pulling your engineers off the core roadmap.
> Worth a 20-minute call to see if your use case is a fit? If not, happy to send our production-readiness checklist anyway.
> [Signature with one proof link]

### Cold email: ICP 2 (Operations partner, professional firm)

> **Subject:** Document admin at [Firm]
>
> Hi [Name], firms like [Firm] typically spend hundreds of hours a month reading, extracting and re-keying information from client documents. We build AI workflows that do the first pass, with your team reviewing anything uncertain, and we measure accuracy on your real documents before anything goes live.
> Would it be useful to see how this worked on [type of document]? Twenty minutes, no pitch deck.

### Follow-ups (days 3, 7, 14)
- Day 3: one-line bump + a relevant resource (checklist / short teardown).
- Day 7: different angle: a short insight specific to their business.
- Day 14: polite close-the-loop ("Shall I close the file? Happy to reconnect later in the year").

### Discovery call script (35 minutes)
1. **Context (5 min):** "What prompted you to take this call now?"
2. **Current state (10 min):** "Walk me through how [workflow] works today. Who does it, how long does it take, what volume? What does a mistake cost?"
3. **Past attempts (5 min):** "What have you tried with AI so far? What happened?"
4. **Desired outcome (5 min):** "If this worked perfectly in six months, what would be different? How would you measure it?"
5. **Constraints (5 min):** data location, security requirements, systems, who else decides, budget range, timing.
6. **Next step (5 min):** propose Snapshot/Sprint/Pilot, book the proposal review meeting **before ending the call**.

### Proposal structure (5–7 pages max)
1. Your situation (in their words, with their numbers)
2. The opportunity and the value at stake (their baseline × realistic improvement range)
3. Our approach (phases, weekly demos, evaluation method)
4. **Acceptance metric** and how we measure it
5. Scope, deliverables and **exclusions**
6. Timeline and team
7. Investment: three options (e.g. Pilot only / Pilot + Run Growth / Pilot + Run + second workflow)
8. Risks and how we manage them (data, accuracy, adoption)
9. Why us + relevant proof
10. Next steps and validity (offer valid 14 days)

### Common objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We'll build it ourselves." | "You can, and you should own it long term. We can get the first one into production in six weeks with evals your team inherits, then hand over or stay on as support. What's the cost of your team being pulled off the roadmap for a quarter?" |
| "Too expensive." | Re-anchor on their quantified pain. Then reduce scope, never price. Offer the Starter tier. |
| "Can't we just use ChatGPT / Copilot?" | "Maybe. We'll tell you honestly in the Sprint. Where off-the-shelf works, we recommend it and save you the build." |
| "Data security worries." | Walk through the data-flow diagram, client-owned API accounts, DPA, no training on client data, UK/EU hosting options. |
| "AI isn't accurate enough." | "That's exactly why we measure accuracy on your real data before go-live, keep humans reviewing uncertain cases, and back the Pilot with an acceptance guarantee." |

## 6.6 Compliance for outreach (UK)

- **PECR:** unsolicited marketing emails to **corporate subscribers** (limited companies, LLPs, public bodies) are allowed with a clear identity and an easy opt-out. Emails to **sole traders and some partnerships** are treated like emails to individuals and generally need prior consent. Check the ICO's current direct marketing guidance. [Verified principle; confirm current guidance]
- **UK GDPR:** document a Legitimate Interests Assessment for B2B prospecting, keep a suppression list, record data sources, and honour objections immediately.
- Use a **separate sending domain** for cold outreach, warm it up, and keep volumes modest (≤ 50/day/inbox) to protect deliverability and the main domain.
- Never scrape personal emails. Business contact data only.
