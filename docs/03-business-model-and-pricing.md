## 3.1 The model in one picture: Land → Build → Run → Expand

```
   LAND (paid diagnostic)      BUILD (fixed-price project)       RUN (recurring)            EXPAND
   ─────────────────────       ──────────────────────────       ─────────────────          ──────────
   AI Opportunity Sprint  ──►  Production Pilot / Automation ──► Managed "Run" retainer ──► Next workflow
   Readiness & Governance      AI MVP / Product feature          Fractional Head of AI      from the
   Workshop                    Agent / RAG system                                           Sprint roadmap
   £3.5k–£25k                  £8k–£150k                         £750–£12k / month
```

- **Land** is always paid. Free "discovery" attracts tyre-kickers and trains clients to undervalue thinking. The Sprint fee is **credited against a Build** signed within 30 days, which removes the objection. [Recommendation]
- **Build** is fixed-price against a fixed scope and an acceptance metric. Clients in our ICP strongly prefer fixed price, and it lets us capture the efficiency gains from our reusable delivery kit, instead of passing them on as fewer billable days. [Recommendation]
- **Run** is the profit engine and the reason the business can be valued. AI systems degrade: models change, data drifts, costs creep, prompts need tuning. So "Run" is a genuine need, not an add-on. **Every Build proposal includes the Run plan by default (first month included); the client has to opt out.** [Recommendation]
- **Expand**: the Sprint produces a ranked roadmap of 5–10 opportunities. Each delivered Build sells the next.

## 3.3 Three service tiers

The tiers aren't a menu of identical services at three sizes. They match **buyer type, risk tolerance and scope**.

| | **Starter** | **Growth** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **For** | Early-stage founders, small businesses (<30 staff) | Scaling startups, established SMEs (30–250 staff) | Larger organisations, regulated or complex environments |
| **Diagnostic** | Opportunity Sprint (1 workflow area, 1 week): **£3,500** | Opportunity Sprint (whole company, 2 weeks): **£7,500** | AI Strategy & Readiness Programme (4–6 weeks): **£15,000–£25,000** |
| **Build** | Automation Build (one workflow, standard integrations, 3–4 weeks): **£8,000–£15,000** | Production Pilot (agent/RAG/automation, custom integrations, evals, 6 weeks): **£20,000–£45,000** | Multi-workflow or multi-agent programme, client cloud, SSO, audit logging: **£60,000–£150,000+** |
| **Product** | AI MVP Blueprint (architecture + clickable prototype, 2 weeks): **£6,000** | AI MVP / AI feature build (6–10 weeks): **£25,000–£60,000** | AI product platform: scoped individually |
| **Run (managed)** | **£750/month**: monitoring, monthly eval run, minor fixes, email support (2 business days) | **£2,000–£3,500/month**: weekly evals, prompt/model updates, cost optimisation, 4–8 improvement hours, next-business-day support | **£6,000–£12,000/month**: SLA, named engineer, quarterly model review, governance reporting, change requests |
| **Advisory** | n/a | Fractional Head of AI, 2 days/month: **£3,000/month** | Fractional Head of AI, 4 days/month: **£6,000/month** |

**Pass-through costs:** LLM API, hosting and third-party SaaS costs are paid **directly by the client on their own accounts** wherever possible. The client keeps ownership and control, we avoid margin disputes and billing risk, and it strengthens our data-protection position. Where we have to front costs, we recharge at cost plus 10%. [Recommendation]

## 3.4 How the prices were set (and why they're defensible)

1. **Market anchor.** UK median contract rates for AI engineers/consultants sit around **£640–£660/day** (advertised), and placement data puts senior AI engineers at **~£800–£900/day**. [Verified] A Growth Production Pilot at £30k for 6 weeks implies an effective rate of roughly **£1,000–£1,250/day** across ~25–30 delivery days. That's a premium of 20–40% over senior contractor rates, justified by fixed price, the acceptance guarantee, reusable components and the Run handover. [Estimate]
2. **Value anchor.** Proposals always show the client's own numbers. Example: a support team of 8 at ~£35k fully-loaded cost each (~£280k/year). If the AI system resolves or drafts 30% of tickets, the capacity freed is worth ~£84k/year, against a £30k build and £2.5k/month Run. Payback inside 6 months. **Only use the client's real baseline. Never invent ROI.** [Method, not data]
3. **Competitive anchor.** Above no-code agencies ($2k–$15k setups) [Verified, weak sources], well below enterprise consultancies (£100k+ minimums) [Estimate]. Exactly where the positioning says we sit.

### Pricing rules [Recommendation]

- **Never discount the price; reduce the scope.** If a client needs a lower number, remove features or integrations.
- **Founding-client offer (first 3 clients only):** 20% off the Build in exchange for a named case study, a reference call and a video testimonial (with written permission). Time-limited and stated openly.
- **Raise prices** by 10–15% after every 3 delivered case studies, or when the close rate on proposals exceeds 50% (a sign we are too cheap).
- **Quote in GBP for UK clients and in EUR/USD for international clients.** Keep the same value logic, adjusted for local market rates.
- **Register for VAT** when turnover approaches the threshold (currently **£90,000**, check HMRC). Most B2B clients reclaim VAT, so registering early is often harmless and looks more established.
