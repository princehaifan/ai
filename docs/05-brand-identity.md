## 5.1 Naming

**Criteria:** short, pronounceable internationally, not "AI-something" (it'll date and blends into thousands of agencies), suggests craft and measurable results, works as `name.com` / `name.ai` / `name.co.uk`.

| Name | Meaning / rationale | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Northwright** ⭐ recommended working name | "Wright" = a maker (shipwright, playwright), so it signals *we build things*. "North" = direction, true heading, roadmap. Sounds established, British-rooted, and works internationally. | Check for similar marks in software/consulting classes |
| **Plumbline** | The tool that shows true vertical: honesty, measurement, precision | Common word; likely existing businesses |
| **Keelson** | The structural backbone of a ship: stability under load | Less intuitive; spelling |
| **Proofwork** | "Proof over promise": evaluation-first | Could be confused with proofreading |
| **Fathom & Field** | Depth of thinking + work in the field | Longer; "Fathom" is widely used |
| **Groundwork Applied AI** | Foundations, practical | Generic; "Groundwork" is a common charity name |

The documents use **"Northwright"** as the working name. Replace it everywhere once checks are complete.

**Name-check steps (half a day):**
1. Companies House name search (UK). 2. UKIPO trademark search (classes 9, 35, 42) and EUIPO/WIPO if international. 3. Domain availability (.com, .ai, .co.uk). 4. LinkedIn, X and GitHub handles. 5. Google search for confusingly similar consultancies. 6. Say it on the phone to five people: can they spell it?

## 5.4 Brand personality

**The senior engineer who is also good at business.** Calm, precise, candid and commercially minded. Never breathless.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Precise | Vague or buzzwordy |
| Candid ("you don't need custom AI for that") | Salesy or hype-driven |
| Commercially grounded (every claim tied to £, hours, risk) | Academic or technology-for-its-own-sake |
| Confident and calm | Arrogant or alarmist ("adapt or die") |
| Accessible: explain without jargon | Condescending |

## 5.5 Core values

1. **Measure what matters.** No metric, no project.
2. **Ship real things.** Production over prototypes, outcomes over output.
3. **Tell the truth.** Including "don't build this" and "buy that instead".
4. **Earn trust with data.** We treat client data as if it were our own reputation, because it is.
5. **Leave clients stronger.** Document everything. Teach as we build. No lock-in.

## 5.6 Messaging framework

| Layer | Message |
|---|---|
| **Core message** | Most AI projects never make it into production. We build the ones that do, and prove they work. |
| **For CTOs (ICP 1)** | Ship the AI features your roadmap promised, with evals, guardrails and cost controls built in, without pulling your team off the core product. |
| **For COOs / Ops (ICP 1 & 2)** | Take the most repetitive workflow off your team's plate in six weeks, with the success metric agreed before we start. |
| **For founders/CEOs** | Know exactly where AI pays off in your business, what to build, what to buy and what it'll cost, in two weeks. |
| **For professional firms** | Use AI on client work safely: a clear policy, the right tools and automations that recover billable hours. |
| **Proof points (fill as earned)** | Case study metrics, eval results, number of systems in production. **Never invent these.** |
| **Words we use** | working, measured, production, evaluation, payback, reliable, practical, accountable |
| **Words we avoid** | revolutionary, game-changing, unleash, supercharge, 10x, magic, "AI-powered everything", disrupt |

## 5.8 Visual identity direction

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Overall feel** | Editorial and engineered: closer to a quality engineering firm or a serious research publication than a typical "AI startup". Lots of white space. Real data visuals instead of stock imagery. |
| **Avoid** | Glowing brains, robots, neural-network blobs, purple-to-blue gradients, circuit boards, stock photos of handshakes. |
| **Colour** | Deep ink navy (primary, trust) · warm off-white (background) · one confident accent (signal orange or deep teal) for calls to action and data highlights · neutral greys. Check WCAG AA contrast. |
| **Typography** | A sharp grotesk for headings (e.g. Inter Tight, Söhne-like) + a highly readable text face; a monospaced face for metrics and code snippets (reinforces "engineered"). |
| **Logo** | Wordmark first. Optional simple mark: a north-pointing chevron or a plumb-line glyph. Must work in one colour at favicon size. |
| **Imagery** | Diagrams of real system architectures, annotated screenshots of dashboards and eval reports (anonymised), founder photography that's natural, not corporate. |
| **Data visual style** | Clean before/after charts, consistent colour for "baseline" vs "with AI". This becomes a recognisable signature in case studies and LinkedIn posts. |
